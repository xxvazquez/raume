Changes made: for each of the 102 kanji, the stroke paths (in stroke order)
and the positions of the stroke numbers were extracted from KanjiVG's
kanji/<codepoint>.svg files into one JavaScript data file. The paths and
positions themselves are unchanged; the other SVG markup and metadata were
left out.
