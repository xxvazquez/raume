How this works

Four reference pages to read, search and quiz yourself on, plus Flashcards — spaced-repetition review of the same words, with its own Help tab. Tap a topic below.

Finding words

Search, jump to a table, sort
  • Search looks in Japanese, kana, romaji and English across every section at once, best match first. Press / to jump to the box.
  • Each result has a small + that adds the word to Flashcards (it turns into a green ✓; tap again to pause the word).
  • Jump to a table, above the search box, lists every table in the current section.
  • Sort the English column with the up/down chevron in its header. Tables start A–Z.

Reading and self-testing

Furigana, Options, particles, badges
  • Furigana sits above the kanji. Tap any kana to see its romaji, or tap the word itself for its full romaji line.
  • The Options button beside the search box has switches to hide Japanese, Furigana or English, and Cover answers, which blanks the English so you can tap a row to check it (resets on reload). A dot on the button means something is switched on.
  • Show polite (in Options, on verb tables) swaps the dictionary form for the 〜ます form.
  • Every small badge beside a row with something to say — a particle, a verb's 五段/一段/変格, an outlined (irregular) adjective — opens the same popover on tap: one line about just that badge, never a permanent line under the meaning.
  • Blue chips beside a verb (and 好き, 嫌い, 上手, 下手) show the particle it takes — tap one to see what it marks (聞く: tap を for what you listen to, tap に for who you ask — never both at once).
  • A small い or な badge marks how an adjective conjugates — the letter itself says which, so a plain one doesn't tap to anything. An outlined one is irregular; tap it to see why (きれい, 嫌い, 有名, or いい → よくない). The legend is at the foot of Options.
  • In the Verbs table, a small 五段 / 一段 / 変格 badge marks the verb's group (godan / ichidan / irregular). Tap it for the reading (they're kanji) and, for an outlined one, why it's worth a second look (切る and 帰る conjugate godan despite looking ichidan; 来る's own reading changes: 来る is kuru, 来ます is kimasu).
  • The speaker icon plays a word's pronunciation. Particles (は, を, から …) show in blue bold — tap one to see how it's read (は as "wa").
  • Phrases are question/answer pairs, in their own order rather than A–Z.

Getting around a section

Open, expand, print
  • A section opens with every table collapsed. Tap a title to open it (others in that category close), or use Expand all / Collapse all in Options.
  • A section remembers which tables you left open.
  • The printer icon on a table prints just that table as clean A4. Print this section and Print whole reference, in Options, do more.

Making it yours

Hide, customize, add words, sync
  • The eye icon at the end of a row hides a word you already know; Show all brings them back.
  • The sliders icon in the top bar opens Customize: rename tables, change their icons and colours, reorder tables and categories, and add your own vocabulary.
  • Your vocabulary takes one word at a time, or a paste / CSV of japanese(furigana),romaji,english — write each kanji's reading in brackets right after it: 帰(かえ)る. Signed in, you can also build whole tables of your own; each word or table can be edited or deleted (both ask first).
  • A table's ⋯ menu has Choose icon… (icon and colour — a table you create picks an icon from its name until you choose one) and Add to flashcards.
  • Sign in on the Flashcards page to sync flashcards, customisations and your own words across devices. Everything also works signed out, saved in this browser — Back up & restore in Flashcards › Settings saves that copy to a file.
  • The person icon in the top bar shows a dot once you're signed in: green when everything is synced, amber if you're offline or something hasn't reached your account yet. A change made offline is saved on this device and syncs automatically.
