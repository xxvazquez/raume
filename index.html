Flashcards

How this works

Four reference sections, plus Flashcards for spaced-repetition review. Tap a topic.

Finding words

Search, tables, sort
  • SearchJapanese, kana, romaji or English, every section at once. Press / to jump in.
  • + on a resultAdds the word to Flashcards. Tap the ✓ again to pause it.
  • TablesLists every table in the section — tap one to go there.
  • SortThe chevron in the English header sorts A–Z or Z–A.

Reading

Furigana, romaji, audio, badges
  • FuriganaAlways shown above the kanji.
  • RomajiTap a word for its romaji. Tap any katakana for that sound alone.
  • SpeakerPlays the word aloud.
  • ParticlesBlue and bold, like は — tap one to see how it reads.
  • Badgesい / な, 五段 / 一段 / 変格, and the particles a verb takes. Tap for a one-line note; an outlined badge is an exception.
  • PhrasesEach sentence with its English underneath, in question-and-answer order.

Options

Show, cover, polite, print
  • Japanese · Furigana · EnglishSwitch any of them off. A dot on Options means something is changed.
  • Cover answersBlanks the English — tap a row to check yourself.
  • Show politeVerb tables switch to the 〜ます form.
  • Expand allOpens every table in the section.
  • PrintOne table (its ⋯ menu), the section, or everything, as A4.

Making it yours

Hide, customize, your words, sync
  • Hide a rowThe eye icon. Show all brings rows back.
  • Hide a tableA table’s ⋯ menu › Hide table. Customize › Show brings it back.
  • CustomizeRename, recolour and reorder tables — the sliders icon at the top.
  • Your vocabularyAdd words one by one or paste a list: 帰(かえ)る,kaeru,to return.
  • SyncSign in on Flashcards. The dot on the person icon: green synced, amber waiting.
  • Back upSigned out? Flashcards › Settings › Back up & restore.
