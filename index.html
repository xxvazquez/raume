Flashcards

How this works

The Vocabulary, Grammar, Phrases, and Travel pages are one big reference you can read, search, and quiz yourself against. Flashcards is a separate spaced-repetition trainer built on the same words, with its own Help tab.

Finding words

  • Search matches Japanese, kana readings, romaji, and English across every section at once, best match first. Press / to jump to the box. Each result also shows a small + that adds that word to Flashcards (it turns into a green ✓ once it's in; tap it again to pause the word).
  • Jump to a table, above the search box, lists every table in the current section.
  • The English column sorts with the up/down chevron in its header. Tables start sorted A–Z by English.

Reading and self-testing

  • Furigana sits above the kanji. Hover or tap any kana to see its romaji.
  • Click or tap the Japanese word or sentence itself to see its full romaji reading underneath.
  • The Japanese / Furigana / English segments hide those columns (or just the readings) in any combination — a raised segment is showing, a struck-through one is hidden. Cover one side and test yourself.
  • The Cover answers switch blanks the English column and shows a reminder to tap a row to reveal that one answer (tap again to re-hide). It resets when you reload.
  • On the Verbs table (and for 好き, 嫌い, 上手, 下手) small blue chips show the particle each word takes — を eat, に/へ go, が like — with a short note under the meaning when it isn't a plain を.
  • The Show polite switch, on verb tables, swaps the dictionary form for the 〜ます form.
  • Where a word is an い- or な-adjective, a small い or な badge follows it to mark how it conjugates; a legend in the toolbar explains them whenever the table on screen actually has one. An outlined badge marks an irregular one — a な-adjective that ends in い (きれい, 嫌い, 有名) or いい, which becomes よくない — with a short note under its meaning.
  • The small speaker icon next to a word plays its pronunciation, using a natural native-voice recording where one exists.
  • Grammatical particles (は, を, から …) show in blue and bold wherever Japanese text appears, including flashcards. Hover or tap one to see how it's read (は as "wa", へ as "e").
  • Phrases tables are laid out as question/answer pairs, in their own order rather than A-Z.

Getting around a section

  • A section opens with every table collapsed; click a title to open it (others in that category close). Expand all opens everything at once, Collapse all closes it back down.
  • A section remembers which tables you left open when you navigate away and back.
  • The printer icon on a table prints just that table as a clean A4 sheet. Print…, by Expand all, does the whole section or the whole reference at once.

Making it yours

  • The eye icon at the end of a row hides words you already know; Show all brings them back.
  • The sliders icon in the top bar opens Customize — rename tables, give them icons, reorder tables and categories, and add your own vocabulary. Everything there is collapsed by default, including Vocabulary's whole group of categories; click a heading to open it, and what you leave open stays open even after you reload the page. Explanatory text sits behind a small ⓘ button next to a heading instead of staying on the page all the time.
  • Your vocabulary (on the Customize page) adds words the built-in tables don't have — one at a time, or a paste / CSV file of japanese(furigana),romaji,english (write each kanji's reading in parentheses right after it: 帰(かえ)る). Rows that don't parse are skipped and listed. Signed in, you can also build tables of your own. Under Words you've added, each table you've touched is its own row you open to edit or delete a word (both ask for confirmation first), or delete the whole table — search and A–Z sort cover every word you've added, whichever table it's in.
  • A table's ⋯ menu holds Choose icon… (same picker as Customize) alongside Add to flashcards, which sends every row in that table to the Flashcards trainer; add or remove individual words from Flashcards' own Manage tab.
  • Sign in on the Flashcards page to sync your flashcards, table customisations, and your own vocabulary across devices. Everything works signed out too, saved in this browser. Signed out, Flashcards › Settings › Back up & restore saves that browser copy to a file (and restores it), since clearing site data would otherwise lose it. The person icon in the top bar carries a small dot once you're signed in — green once everything's synced, amber if you're offline or something hasn't reached your account yet (hover it for specifics). A change made offline is never lost: it's saved on this device right away and syncs automatically the next time you're connected.
