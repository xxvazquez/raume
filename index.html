Flashcards

How this works

The Vocabulary, Grammar, and Travel pages are one big reference you can read, search, and quiz yourself against. Flashcards is a separate spaced-repetition trainer built on the same words, with its own Help tab.

Finding words

  • Search matches Japanese, kana readings, romaji, and English across every section at once, best match first. Press / to jump to the box.
  • Jump to a table, above the search box, lists every table in the current section.
  • Each column sorts with the ↕ control in its header. Tables start sorted A–Z by English.

Reading and self-testing

  • Furigana sits above the kanji. Hover or tap any kana to see its romaji.
  • The Japanese / Furigana / Romaji / English buttons hide those columns (or just the readings) in any combination — cover one side and test yourself.
  • Cover answers blanks the English column and shows a reminder to tap a row to reveal that one answer (tap again to re-hide). It resets when you reload.
  • The ★ at the end of a row stars it — a "keep an eye on this" list. Once you've starred anything, a Starred button appears in the toolbar and collects those words into one sortable, printable table. Stars sync with your account.
  • Show polite, on verb tables, swaps the dictionary form for the 〜ます form.
  • Where a word is an い- or な-adjective, a small い-adj or な-adj tag next to its meaning marks how it conjugates.
  • The small speaker icon next to a word plays its pronunciation. It only appears once your browser confirms it has a Japanese voice installed.

Getting around a section

  • A section opens with every table collapsed; click a title to open it (others in that category close). Expand all opens everything at once, Collapse all closes it back down.
  • A section remembers which tables you left open when you navigate away and back.
  • The printer icon on a table prints just that table as a clean A4 sheet. Print…, by Expand all, does the whole section or the whole reference at once.

Making it yours

  • The eye icon at the end of a row hides words you already know; Show all brings them back.
  • The sliders icon in the top bar opens Customize — rename tables, give them icons, reorder tables and categories, and add your own vocabulary.
  • Your vocabulary (on the Customize page) adds words the built-in tables don't have — one at a time, or a paste / CSV file of japanese(furigana),romaji,english (write each kanji's reading in parentheses right after it: 帰(かえ)る). Rows that don't parse are skipped and listed. Signed in, you can also build tables of your own.
  • Add to flashcards (a table's ⋯ menu, or the button at the end of a row) sends words to the Flashcards trainer.
  • Sign in on the Flashcards page to sync your flashcards and your table customisations across devices. Everything works signed out too, saved in this browser.
